# Plan entitlements

Billing.Contracts.PlanCatalog is the canonical source of monthly PLN prices, limits and paid capabilities. New checkouts use Free/Starter/Pro/Team/Studio at 0/39/99/299/699 PLN; Team and Studio buy the whole workspace (3/10 seats), with no extra-seat checkout. Existing paid periods, accepted prices, payment snapshots and data are preserved; renewal uses the canonical price for the next period. Upgrades begin a new paid period without proration. Downgrades and cancellation apply at period end. All charges/refunds are simulated and auditable.

Access combines role, operational configuration and paid features; resource ownership remains in the owning modules. Plan overrides cannot grant a missing paid entitlement. Team seats may grant professional tools, but quotas are explicitly Personal or Team; membership alone never silently spends a workspace pool. `/me/usage?teamId=...` validates membership and returns nullable unlimited limits, usage and period dates. Free periods use UTC calendar months; paid periods use persisted billing dates.

Access usage reservations are unique by owner/limit/operation across periods. PostgreSQL advisory locks serialize admission for the whole scope. Successful operations settle once, failures release, and expired holds stop consuming capacity. Local results consume a permit once per result ID. The controlled web flow reserves before handing a clip to the companion; old clients authorize on synchronization. A fully offline/uncontrolled local executable cannot be cryptographically prevented from running, but cannot synchronize unlimited successful results.

Generator previews/reshuffles consume only successful feasible runs. Identical seeded retries reuse their receipt; direct save also verifies the preview receipt. Manual programs and copies are not generator runs. Client activation/reactivation uses one capacity lock and distinct active clients; Team capacity counts active client cases. Downgrades preserve existing relationships and members and block new admission above the allowance.

Cloud admission and worker completion require an explicitly registered trusted GPU meter. Video length is not GPU usage. Holds transfer to fenced worker leases; completion settles measured synchronized device work in seconds, while failures/cancellation release the hold. No real GPU-meter adapter is currently configured, so cloud execution returns `CLOUD_ANALYSIS_UNAVAILABLE`; no synthetic result is supplied. Technical upload/concurrency/rate limits continue to apply on every plan.

The separate worker composes only Billing's entitlement reader and Access's usage reservations, not payment services. Its role can read the required entitlement columns and mutate reservation rows; payments, payment methods, account profiles, Auth and training documents remain inaccessible.
